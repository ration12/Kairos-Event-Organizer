This Privacy Policy applies to the Kairos - Make It Count mobile application and related services operated by Kairos Collective Insight LLC (collectively, the “Application”).

Information Collection and Use

The Application may collect information when you download and use it, including:

  • Your device’s Internet Protocol address
  • The pages of the Application you visit, including the time and date of your visit and time spent on those pages
  • The time spent using the Application
  • Your mobile operating system

The Service Provider may use information you provide to send important information, required notices, and, where permitted by law, marketing communications. For a better experience, the Application may require you to provide certain personally identifiable information. Information requested will be retained and used as described in this Policy.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use cookies, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies to support functionality, analytics, or service delivery. Where required by applicable law, the Service Provider will obtain consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data held by the Service Provider. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise these rights, contact karvi_29@yahoo.com.

Your California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and receive non-discriminatory treatment for exercising these rights. To exercise your CCPA/CPRA rights, contact the Service Provider.

Third-Party Access

Only aggregated, anonymized data is periodically transmitted to external services to help the Service Provider improve the Application and its service. The Service Provider may disclose User Provided and Automatically Collected Information:

  • As required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process
  • When disclosure is believed in good faith to be necessary to protect rights or safety, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request
  • To trusted service providers working on the Service Provider’s behalf, who have no independent use of the disclosed information and have agreed to follow this Policy

International Data Transfers

The Service Provider or its third-party service providers may transfer personal data outside your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area. Where required, appropriate safeguards may include:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy decisions or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms
  • Your consent, where required and legally permitted

Data protection laws in other countries may differ from those in your jurisdiction. The Service Provider will apply safeguards and obtain consent where required by law.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop further collection from your mobile device by uninstalling the Application. Uninstalling stops future collection from the device but does not automatically delete information already transmitted to the Service Provider or third parties.

To request deletion, withdraw consent, or exercise any of your rights, contact karvi_29@yahoo.com.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider retains personal data based on its necessity for the stated purposes:

  • User Provided Data: for the duration of your use plus 12 months, unless longer retention is required by law
  • Automatically Collected Data: for up to 24 months from collection, unless longer retention is required for legal compliance
  • Aggregated and Anonymized Data: indefinitely, because it no longer identifies you
  • Legal compliance data: for as long as applicable law requires

You may request deletion, subject to legal retention obligations. Some User Provided Data may be required for the Application to function properly.

Children

The Application is not intended for children under 13, or a higher age where required by applicable law. The Service Provider does not knowingly solicit personal data from children or market the Application to them.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to monitor children’s internet use and instruct them not to submit personally identifiable information without permission. If you believe a child has provided such information, contact the Service Provider so appropriate action can be taken. Where permitted by law, users under 13 require consent from a parent or guardian.

Security

The Service Provider uses physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards designed to protect the confidentiality of information it processes and maintains.

Data Breach Notification

If a data breach affects your personal data, the Service Provider will provide notice in accordance with applicable legal requirements, including information about the nature of the breach and steps being taken to address it where required.

Changes to This Policy

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by posting the updated Policy with a new effective date. Where required by law, consent will be requested before material changes take effect.

Previous versions are available upon request by contacting the Service Provider.

Your Consent

Where processing is based on consent, you provide consent by affirmatively opting in to the relevant feature or action. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal. Processing based on other lawful bases is carried out as described above.
